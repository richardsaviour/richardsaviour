Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This page explains what information this website collects, why, and what you can do about it. The site is run by Richard Saviour ("I", "me").

1. What I collect

  • Your email address, when you give it to the Robin assistant or email me.
  • Your chat messages with Robin, and the page you were on.
  • What you send me by email or share on a call.

I do not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies on this site.

2. Why I collect it

  • To answer your questions.
  • To follow up with you about your project by email.
  • To improve Robin's answers.

I do not sell your information. I do not add you to a mailing list without asking.

3. How Robin works

  • Robin asks for your email before answering, so I can follow up with you.
  • Your email is remembered in your own browser so Robin does not ask again. Clearing your browser data removes it.
  • Your messages are sent to my server and then to an AI model provider to write the reply. Please do not type passwords, card numbers or other sensitive details into the chat.

4. Other services this site uses

These services may receive basic technical data such as your IP address when the site loads:

  • GitHub Pages (hosting)
  • Google Fonts, cdnjs and jsDelivr (fonts and scripts)
  • Cloudinary and other image hosts (videos and images)
  • Calendly, if you book a call
  • A payment provider, if you pay by card. I never see or store your card number.

5. How long I keep it

I keep emails and chat records for as long as they are useful for our work together or for following up, and delete them when you ask.

6. Your choices

This site is run from Nigeria, and your rights under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 apply. You can ask me to show you, correct or delete the information I hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Email richardsaviourz@gmail.com and I will sort it out.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
