03 / How I can help

I don't just hand over the file.

Most people who make the asset stop there. The gap between "the video is done" and "the video is working" is where a lot of time quietly disappears.

01

Plan

What it's for, who it's for, and what it needs to do.

02

Make

The edit, the motion, the design, the build.

03

Revise

Your notes, properly applied. Not grudgingly.

04

Publish

Scheduled and posted, so it doesn't sit in a folder.

05

Improve

Read the numbers. Change what they say isn't working.

If you need content

You can hand me footage and get an edit back. That's the small version.

The useful version is that I can sit inside the whole loop — planning what gets made, making it, publishing it, then telling you what the analytics actually mean and what I'd change next month.

If something's slow

I don't turn up selling automation. I look at how you already work and ask the boring questions.

What's taking too long? What gets typed twice? What could be talking to what? What's worth automating — and what honestly isn't? Then we fix the ones that pay for themselves.

Multiple revision rounds included. Though if I got it right the first time, we won't be needing many.
